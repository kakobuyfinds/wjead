What to check before ordering

  • Match the seller and SKU

    Open the HipobuyFind item, locate the seller link, and confirm the listing and variant when opening it in your agent account. Compare the seller’s measurements with a garment you own.

  • Review warehouse QC photos

    Check the color, size label, stitching and any measurements visible in your warehouse photos. Check the return window while the item is still at the warehouse.

  • Estimate the delivered cost

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Use your destination and expected parcel dimensions when comparing shipping lines.

Frequently asked questions

Does the spreadsheet price include shipping?

The table shows the listed item price in CNY. Confirm delivery charges and any applicable duties for your destination before paying.

Are the product photos warehouse QC photos?

Use listing photos to compare styles and review the warehouse QC photos for your specific order when your agent makes them available.

What if a product link has changed?

Search the item ID or product name on HipobuyFind, then check the seller listing and your preferred size or color again.
